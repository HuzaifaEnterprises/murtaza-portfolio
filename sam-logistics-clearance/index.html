A shipment rarely gets stuck at the port. It gets stuck because one paper was late. This desk sheet tracks every document a consignment needs, shows which ones block the Goods Declaration or the delivery order, and projects the release day and the demurrage if something is still missing. Tick a document, change the channel or the free days, and the plan updates.

How I'd use this at SAM Logistics

  1. Open a file like this for every inbound job the day the booking is confirmed, and chase the shipper for anything that blocks the GD before the vessel arrives. I do this for our own timber shipments from Finland and Sweden, where the phytosanitary and treatment papers decide whether a container sits at the port.
  2. Send each client a short status update from the same file: what is in hand, what is missing, and the projected gate-out day. I have built automations that send updates like this without anyone typing them.
  3. Log the delay on every job against its cause. After a quarter, the data shows which shippers, lines or document types cost the most days, and that is where the follow-up effort goes.
