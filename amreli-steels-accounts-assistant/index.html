Duty 1

Post customer collections after verification against bank statements

I would never post a receipt before it shows on the bank statement with the same amount and reference. At Huzaifa Enterprises I run customer ledgers every day, and my app opened every ledger for the first time, which is how over $10K in forgotten balances came to light.

Duty 2

Verify online payments and provide payment confirmations

Check amount, reference, date and customer, then confirm to Sales the same day. A confirmation only goes out after the money is seen. The sample desk below runs exactly this check.

Duty 3

Report daily collection status to management

One short report at the end of each day: collected and verified, on hold, disputed, unidentified. I already report profitability, cash conversion and average payment days (DSO) to management, so a daily view is a natural step.

Still to learn: what your management wants in the report.

Duty 4

Coordinate with Sales and banks to resolve receipt disputes

Every exception gets an owner and a next step: Sales for who paid what, the bank for what actually landed. I work with suppliers and customers in English every day, and I keep following up until an item is closed.

Still to learn: your escalation path and bank contacts.

Duty 5

Obtain bank statements for reconciliation

I own the monthly P&L, income statement and COGS report at Alzo Home, in Excel and Sheets, to a deadline. I know how a missing item breaks a month-end.

Still to learn: full bank reconciliation at your size. This is the biggest gap and I would ask for guidance early.
