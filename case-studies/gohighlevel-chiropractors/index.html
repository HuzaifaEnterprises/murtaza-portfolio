Case study

GoHighLevel automation for chiropractors: 15+ workflows for US clinics

I worked under Eric Smith, a US chiropractor, on GoHighLevel automation for chiropractic clinics. Across 5 clinics I built more than 15 workflows using GoHighLevel, n8n, Make and Zapier.

  • 15+workflows built
  • 5US chiropractic clinics
  • 1 hour to 5 minutesclient onboarding
The problem

Old patients never contacted, appointments missed, every client set up by hand

The clinics were losing patients in quiet ways. People who had visited once were never contacted again. Appointments were missed with no follow-up. And each new client had to be set up by hand, which took about an hour every time.

What I built

Workflows that follow up so staff don't have to

  • Patient reactivation automation: messages to past patients who had gone quiet
  • Nurturing sequences: follow-ups for new leads until they book
  • Appointment booking and reminders
  • No-show follow-ups
  • Review requests after a visit
  • Automated intake and setup for each new client
The result

Onboarding in 5 minutes, with a weekly report

Setting up a new client used to take an hour of manual work. It now takes about 5 minutes, and a weekly report goes out without anyone preparing it.
