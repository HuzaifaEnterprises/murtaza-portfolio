Case study

Import Desk Pro: import business management software for a timber importer

Huzaifa Enterprises imports softwood timber into Karachi from sawmills in Finland and Sweden. I built them an invoicing and ledger app that replaced handwritten invoices and a diary. It took 4 days to go from the first line of code to daily use.

The problem

100+ customers, 3 staff, and a diary

Every invoice was written by hand. Who had paid, who hadn't, and who was overdue lived in a diary. Three people kept the records for more than 100 customers.

To answer a simple question like "when did this customer last pay?", someone had to dig through pages. Hours went on writing invoices, chasing payments, recording ledgers and reconciling them. And nobody could see the full picture of what the business was owed.

What I built

One app for invoices, ledgers, payments and documents

Import Desk Pro covers the daily work of an import business:

  • Invoices and receipts
  • A ledger for every customer: what they owe, what's due, when they last paid
  • Payments to sawmills
  • Shipping documents

It also reports three numbers the owners had never seen in one place: monthly profitability, the cash conversion cycle, and the average number of days customers take to pay.

I built it with Supabase, GitHub, Claude and Vercel.

The result

Over $10K found, and 20+ hours a week back

When all the ledgers were opened up in one place, the company found over $10K in long-unpaid customer balances. They had been noted in the diary and forgotten.

The team now saves 20+ hours a week on writing invoices, chasing payments, recording ledgers, reconciling, and checking who has paid and who hasn't.
