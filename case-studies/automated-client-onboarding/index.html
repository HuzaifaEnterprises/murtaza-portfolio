Case study

Automated client onboarding for agencies: a system for Gnrich Studios

Gnrich Studios is an agency. When a new client signs, the same steps have to happen every time. I put them into one flow in Make: a form, a contract, a team alert, a welcome email and a live dashboard.

The problem

Contracts going back and forth, checklists sent by hand

If you run an agency, you know how tough client onboarding gets. Contracts go back and forth. Someone sends the onboarding checklist by hand. And the sheet that tracks it all is the thing that gets forgotten.

What I built

One form starts everything

  • A new client fills in the onboarding form: name, company, phone, email and contract amount
  • The team gets an alert in Discord to review and approve the contract before it goes to the client
  • The contract is filled in from the form details and sent for e-signature
  • When the client signs, they get a welcome email with three next steps: join Discord, book an onboarding call, and check their email for access
  • A Google Sheets dashboard updates itself: total clients, signed, pending and emails sent

The whole flow runs in Make.

Inside the system

Screens from my walkthrough. They use test data, and the contract is a demo.

The result

Every client onboarded the same way

Every new client now goes through the same steps, and nothing waits on someone remembering. The dashboard shows who has signed, who is pending and who has had their welcome email.
