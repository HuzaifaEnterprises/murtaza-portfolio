Case study

AI video editing for online courses: a 27-course program for Tarot Times With Heidi

Heidi runs Tarot Times With Heidi in Vancouver, Canada. Their Psychic Training School is a program of 27 courses. I'm handling the online course production from end to end: editing, transcripts, workbooks, YouTube and the Wix course pages.

Visit tarottimeswithheidi.caOpen the app I built
  • 7K+project
  • 27courses: 25 recorded and being updated, 2 new
  • In progressStatus
The problem

A finished course Heidi didn't want to publish

Heidi had recorded a course and wasn't happy with it. On camera they looked distracted in places, as if reading from notes. Their plan was to record the whole course again.

What I did

Edit it, don't re-record it

I edited the recording with AI tools and gave it consistent branding. The result looked more premium than the original, and Heidi didn't have to record it again.

Heidi liked it enough to ask for the same across the entire program. The work now covers:

  • AI video editing for every course
  • Transcripts
  • Workbooks
  • YouTube uploads
  • Wix online course setup and splash pages

I also built Heidi a private client portal with the agreement, payment timeline, progress tracker and weekly updates, plus an app for their site.

The result so far

Hours of re-recording saved, and a stronger brand

The project is still in progress, so there are no final numbers yet. What has changed already:

  • Heidi kept the original recordings and skipped re-recording a full course
  • The courses and the brand around them look more premium and consistent
