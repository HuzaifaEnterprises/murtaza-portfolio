About Murtaza Mustafa

I'm a business operations and AI automation specialist based in Karachi, Pakistan. I have a Business Management degree and more than 3 years of hands-on work in operations, procurement, e-commerce and process automation, all of it built alongside full-time study.

My story

It started because I was lazy

I was studying for a business degree when AI tools took off. Honestly, I was too lazy to do my assignments the slow way, so I started using AI to get them done.

Then I used it for my projects. Then for real work. Assignments turned into automations, automations turned into full apps, and the apps turned into paying clients, including a 7K+ project.

The lazy part never left. I still look at every task and ask why a person is doing it by hand. That question is most of my job.

What I bring

Business judgement first, then the build

I understand pricing, suppliers and margins because I've worked with them: buying timber from sawmills in Finland and Sweden, owning a monthly profit and loss, running my own business. So I automate where it saves real time and money, and leave alone what works.
