3 Things I'd Automate for Rezac & Associates

From your public website
i.

Online booking requests

What I noticed on your site

The homepage Book Now, Click To Call and Call Today buttons all dial (719) 465-1502. There is no online scheduler.

rezacpt.com, homepage
What it changes for you

Patients who find you in the evening can book then, instead of trying to remember to call. Fewer calls for the front desk.

ii.

Digital intake and insurance capture

What I noticed on your site

The Patient Forms page asks new patients to complete the intake packet (about 20 to 30 minutes), fax page 1 for insurance verification and bring the rest.

rezacpt.com/paperwork
What it changes for you

Clean, typed answers arrive before the visit. Insurance is verified early, and nobody re-keys handwriting.

iii.

Reminders and follow-up

What I noticed on your site

The only form on the homepage is a newsletter sign-up, and no visit reminders are mentioned.

rezacpt.com, homepage
What it changes for you

Fewer missed visits, and a missed one is rebooked the same day, so plans of care keep moving.
