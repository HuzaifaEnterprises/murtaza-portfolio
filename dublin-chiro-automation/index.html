3 Things I'd Automate

Based on your public website
I

Online booking calendar

What I noticed on your site

The "Schedule Your Appointment!" button opens a pre-filled email to DrJamie@DublinFamilyChiropractic.com with the subject "New Appointment". Every request waits for a manual reply, and after-hours visitors cannot book at all.

Source: dublinfamilychiropractic.com, homepage
What it changes for you

Patients pick a day and time from your live calendar, any hour. They get an instant confirmation with directions to 6365 Shier Rings Rd, and you see the booking in your schedule without replying to an email.

II

Digital new patient intake

What I noticed on your site

The New Patient Center page offers 6 PDF downloads: patient information, consent, HIPAA, privacy acknowledgement, cancellation policy and health history. Patients must download, print, fill in by hand, and bring them to the clinic.

Source: dublinfamilychiropractic.com, New Patient Center
What it changes for you

All six forms become one secure online form sent automatically after booking. Details arrive complete, typed and in your system before the patient walks in. No printing, no scanning, no re-keying.

III

Reminders and no-show follow-up

What I noticed on your site

There are no visible appointment reminders or follow-up flows on the site. Patients who finish their plan of care or drop off are likely not invited back unless someone calls.

Source: dublinfamilychiropractic.com, all pages reviewed
What it changes for you

Text and email reminders before each visit cut no-shows. A missed appointment triggers an automatic rebooking message the same day, and patients who have not visited in a set period get a friendly check-in.
