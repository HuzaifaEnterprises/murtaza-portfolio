3 Things I'd Automate

Based on your public website
I

Self-serve booking by location

What I noticed on your site

The appointment form on your Contact page says "Use the form to request an appointment. We will call you to schedule." Every request waits for a callback, and phone tag is common when patients are at work.

Source: dynamicptcos.com, Contact page
What it changes for you

Patients pick North, South or Pueblo and a time that suits them, day or night, and get an instant confirmation. Your front desk sees the booking already in the calendar instead of starting a call.

II

Online new patient intake

What I noticed on your site

Your New Patients page says "You may simply arrive 30 minutes early to your first appointment to fill out paperwork", or call to have the forms sent ahead.

Source: dynamicptcos.com, New Patients page
What it changes for you

Intake and insurance forms go out automatically the moment a visit is booked, with a nudge if they are not done in 48 hours. Patients arrive ready, and the first visit starts on time.

III

Reminders and missed-visit rebooking

What I noticed on your site

The patient pages do not mention appointment reminders, and there is no visible way to rebook online after a missed session. Plans of care depend on patients keeping every visit.

Source: dynamicptcos.com, Patients and Contact pages
What it changes for you

Text and email reminders before each session, and an automatic rebooking message after a no-show, so fewer visits are lost and plans of care stay on track without staff chasing.
