why an upgrade matters for tarot times with heidi

Your courses are a quiet, trusting space. People arrive curious and sometimes nervous. The video is the first thing they feel.

  • Easier to follow. Clear captions and clean sound help people who watch on a phone, without headphones, or who simply take in words better when they can read them too.
  • More you, less reading from a page. Tighter pacing and gentle on-screen prompts let your warmth lead, and the notes stay out of sight.
  • Feels like one world. Same colours, same handwriting, same Futura captions in every course, so a student moving from one course to the next feels they are still at the same kitchen table.
  • Calmer for students. A steady, in-focus background and consistent audio levels stop the small distractions that pull attention away from the teaching.
  • Less of your time. You keep teaching and reading. I handle the polishing, so you are not spending evenings fighting editing software.

I cannot promise a number for sales or completion rates, and I will not invent one. What I can say is that clearer, warmer videos remove reasons for a student to feel lost, and that is where satisfaction starts.
