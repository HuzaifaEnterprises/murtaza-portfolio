Three quotes, one defensible PO

A quote comparison I would run before asking for purchase order approval on a technical item. It turns three supplier quotes into landed cost, adjusts for credit terms, and ranks them on a weighted score you can change.

Effective cost = landed cost − value of paying later (landed cost × cost of capital × credit days ÷ 365). Each factor is scored against the best quote, then weighted. Weights are normalised to 100%.

How I'd use this in the role

  • Before raising a PO for technical or indirect materials, compare at least three quotes on landed cost, lead time and terms, so the approval request carries its own cost-benefit case. I did the same when buying softwood from sawmills in Finland and Sweden, where freight, duty and payment terms changed the real price.
  • Feed the chosen supplier's credit days straight into the supplier payment report. At Huzaifa Enterprises I built an app that tracks sawmill payments, ledgers and invoices, and reports DSO and cash conversion cycle every month.
  • Use the score history to make the case for developing a new supplier when an incumbent keeps losing on lead time or price. I onboarded 2 new suppliers this way.
