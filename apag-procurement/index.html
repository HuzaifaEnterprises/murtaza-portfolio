Before a PO for a technical item goes for approval, I put every quote on the same basis: landed cost including freight and duty, adjusted for the value of credit terms, then scored on cost, lead time, terms and warranty. Change any figure or weight and the award updates.

Landed = unit price × quantity + freight and duty. Effective = landed − landed × cost of capital × credit days ÷ 365. Each factor is scored against the best quote, then weighted.

How I'd use this in the role

  1. Attach this sheet to every PO request for technical and indirect materials, so the approval carries its own cost-benefit case. I compared sawmill offers from Finland and Sweden the same way, where freight, duty and payment terms changed the real price.
  2. Carry the awarded supplier's credit days straight into the supplier payment report. At Huzaifa Enterprises I built the system that tracks sawmill payments and reports DSO and the cash conversion cycle each month.
  3. Use the score history to justify developing a new supplier when an incumbent keeps losing on lead time or price. I onboarded 2 new suppliers on that basis.
